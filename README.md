# Chimpanzee · Bonobo · Human — A Field Comparison | 黑猩猩 · 倭黑猩猩 · 人类 — 一份现场对照

A bilingual single-page side-by-side comparison of chimpanzees, bonobos, and humans across 12 dimensions — biology, genetics, cognition, language, social structure, behavior, culture, habitat, ethics, plus a Human-vs-Primate-vs-AI section and a future-evolution module.

## Sections

1. Taxonomy & Ancestry — phylogenetic tree visualization
2. Physical Characteristics — full comparison table
3. Genetics & Evolution — DNA similarity, key human-specific genes
4. Intelligence & Cognition — **interactive radar chart** across 8 dimensions, toggle species
5. Language & Communication — capability comparison table
6. Social Structure — chimp politics vs bonobo diplomacy vs human variation
7. Behavior & Psychology — aggression, sex, bonding
8. Culture & Learning — Tomasello's ratchet effect
9. Environment & Habitat — range, conservation status
10. Ethics & Philosophy — what *actually* defines "human"
11. Human vs Primate vs AI — three-substrate comparison
12. Future Evolution & Intelligence — Pan / Homo / Mind speculation

Plus: 15 cited sources, hover-glossary tooltips throughout, **dark + light mode toggle**, **bilingual EN ↔ 中文**, mobile-responsive.

## Disclaimer

Numerical figures are best-current-consensus values; ranges compress active scientific debate. Curated for accessibility, not academic precision. Sources include de Waal, Tomasello, Goodall, Gallup, Inoue/Matsuzawa, Wrangham, Hare/Woods + IUCN Red List + the published chimp + bonobo genome papers.
